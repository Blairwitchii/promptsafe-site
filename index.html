How it works

Catch it. Redact it. Prove it.

Catch it before it's sent

Pasted or typed card numbers, SSNs, bank details, access keys, and more are flagged on the spot, with checksum checks to keep false alarms low.

Redact in one click

Staff replace sensitive values with safe placeholders like [SSN_1] and keep working. Strict mode removes the "send anyway" option.

Prove you're in control

An audit log of rule names and counts, never content, plus a one-page report for insurance renewals and client security questionnaires.

Straight answers

What it is, and what it isn't.

Does it guarantee compliance?

No. PromptSafe reduces accidental leaks and documents your controls. It does not guarantee protection or legal compliance, and it is not legal advice.

Can it see my prompts?

The extension reads text in the prompt box on supported AI sites, on your device only, to find sensitive patterns. The Free version sends nothing to us, and the Team version uploads counts only.

What does it miss?

It uses pattern and checksum rules, so names and free-form facts in a prompt are not caught yet, and files are not scanned. Treat it as a safety net, not a substitute for a policy.

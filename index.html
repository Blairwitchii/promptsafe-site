Straight answers

Does it guarantee compliance? No. PromptSafe reduces accidental leaks and documents your controls. It does not guarantee protection or legal compliance, and it is not legal advice.

Can it see my prompts? The extension reads text in the prompt box on supported AI sites, on your device only, to find sensitive patterns. It sends nothing to us in the Free version, and the Team version uploads counts only.

What does it miss? It uses pattern and checksum rules, so names and free-form facts in a prompt are not caught yet. Treat it as a safety net, not a substitute for a policy.
